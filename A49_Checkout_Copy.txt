WHY HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP JUSTIFIES A$49
================================================================

You are not paying A$30 extra for a prettier PDF of the same screen.

A$19 already gives you the matching engine: Top 3, scores, why it fits,
and the catalogue’s first actions. That is the automated assessment.

A$49 pays for a reviewer to sit with *your* 12 answers and do four things
the engine does not do on its own:

1. Confirm Rank 1 is the right *start* for this person — not just the
   highest score on a list.
2. Write a day-by-day 7-day checklist that respects their hours and
   budget, instead of leaving three generic actions unsequenced.
3. Stretch those actions into a 30-day milestone pathway with evidence
   checkpoints, so week 2 and week 3 are not improvised.
4. Flag critical risks and watch-outs from what they said they cannot
   spend, cannot risk, or would rather avoid.

That is human-in-the-loop: the engine ranks; a person plans.

If you will do that planning yourself, A$19 is the honest choice.
If you want that planning done from your diagnostic, A$49 is the fit.

Still ranked fits. Not a promise of income. Not financial advice.

REASSURANCE / FAQ — CHOOSING A TIER
================================================================

Which one should I buy?

Choose A$19 if you want the ranked map quickly and are happy to decide
what to do on Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 yourself.

Choose A$49 if you want a reviewer to read your answers and hand you a
7-day checklist and 30-day pathway around Rank 1.

Is A$49 the same report at a higher price?

No. The automated Top 3 is the A$19 product. A$49 adds human review and
the written week-and-month plan. You still get the in-app map; the
Personal Money Map is the extra deliverable.

Can I buy A$19 now and upgrade later?

Treat them as two products. If you only need the engine output, stay on
A$19. If you already know you want the reviewed plan, buy A$49 once.

Will A$49 make me money?

No. Neither tier can promise income. You get a clearer fit (A$19) or a
clearer fit plus a clearer plan (A$49). Earnings depend on your
circumstances, effort, skills, and the market.

Is this financial advice?

No. Matching and planning based on your assessment. Not financial,
investment, tax, or employment advice.

When do I get the A$49 report?

After Stripe confirms a paid A$49 checkout, complete the assessment.
Your automated Top 3 still appears in-app. The Personal Money Map is
prepared from those answers after human review.

What if I only have a little time this week?

That is exactly what the reviewer uses. The 7-day checklist is meant to
fit the hours you stated — not a 40-hour sprint.
